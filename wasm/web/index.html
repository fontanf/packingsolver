Solve cutting and packing problems in your browser. The computation runs on your machine: nothing is sent to a server.

This page needs SharedArrayBuffer, which requires the Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin and Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp headers. Serve it with python3 wasm/web/serve.py.
